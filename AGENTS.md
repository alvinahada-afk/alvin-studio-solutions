## Application rules
- Keep the requested homepage as a single scrolling experience with reusable section and product-preview components; all requested content belongs to the homepage.
- Treat Cafe Flow dashboard data and portfolio websites as illustrative previews, not live products or verified client projects, to avoid claiming working integrations.
- Keep consultation client-side until a real contact destination is supplied; never invent contact details or imply enquiries have been delivered.
- Define all visual styles and semantic tokens in the global stylesheet and use shared Button variants for actions to maintain visual consistency.
